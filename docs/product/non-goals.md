# Non-Goals

Last updated: 2026-10-17

## Purpose

List what the project is intentionally not doing now.

Non-goals protect focus. They also stop agents from reviving old ideas as if
they were active scope.

## Current Non-Goals

The [Current Version Product Contract](./current-version-contract.md) owns
the full current scope and authorization boundary.

| ID | Non-goal | Reason | Revisit trigger | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NG-001 | Built-in AI assistant or autonomous assistant runtime in the current delivery | Owner's 2026-10-17 scope decision; external MCP access is a separate surface. | New owner scope decision and separate safety contract. | Product owner |
| NG-002 | Separate native mobile application in the current delivery | Owner's 2026-10-17 scope decision; responsive web remains in scope. | New owner scope decision. | Product owner |
| NG-003 | Payments or self-service checkout in Soar in the current delivery | Plans are assigned manually by a human administrator in the Soar panel. | New owner scope and payment decision. | Product owner |

## Rule

If work touches a non-goal, stop and create an open decision before
implementation.
